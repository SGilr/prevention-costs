# Rebasing to 2025/26 prices: review for sign-off

Status of `src/data/rebase.json`: **draft**. Nothing rebased appears on the site or in the downloads until the status is `signed-off`, with a name and date.

Generated by `npm run rebase:review` from `rebase.json`, `ledger.json` and `deflators.csv` (HM Treasury GDP deflators, June 2026 Quarterly National Accounts; 2025/26 = 100). Figures are shown to three significant figures, as they would appear on the site.

## Method

- **Financial year:** amount × 100 ÷ financial-year index for the price year (2025/26 = 100).
- **Calendar year:** amount × 100 ÷ (0.25 × index for the financial year ending in March of that calendar year + 0.75 × index for the financial year starting in April), because nine months of a calendar year fall in the later financial year.
- **Rules:** One £ figure per entry, quoted exactly from the value field. Ratios, percentages, counts, DALYs and US$ figures are not rebased. Entries with no stated price year, an approximate year, a range of years, or a year later than the latest deflator outturn are left out.
- **Label on the site:** "Oxon Advisory calculation, 2025/26 prices, HM Treasury GDP deflator"

Entries marked CHECK are the judgement calls most worth a second look.
